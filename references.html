ILLUSTRATION AND MAPS

Visual credits

The illustration and maps help explain the topic in different ways. Their sources are listed below.

Home page illustration

The house and ground-layer illustration was made for this website using SVG. It gives a simple picture of the process.

It does not show an actual property or measured ground conditions in Hagonoy.

Published study map

The image on our Map page was taken from Figure 3, printed page 309, of Maclang et al. (2026).

The paper was published in ISPRS Annals of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences.

The map shows average land deformation velocity in Hagonoy and Calumpit from 2015 to the first quarter of 2021.

The original image, legend, scale, labels, and map credits are retained. No changes were made to the map.

Google location map

The interactive location map is provided by Google Maps. Its attribution appears within the embedded map.

We use it to help visitors locate Hagonoy and nearby places. Its markers do not represent subsidence risk.

ABOUT BANTAY LUPA

Our student project

We created Bantay Lupa for our Environmental Science course to help readers learn about land subsidence in Hagonoy, Bulacan.

The website includes basic explanations, local research, maps, a short quiz, and a sample observation form.

The study map shows past observations. The website does not provide live monitoring, assess individual properties, or send official community reports.
