01

What is land subsidence?

Land subsidence is the settling or sinking of the ground. It can happen slowly over time or suddenly, depending on its cause.

Learn the Basics →
02

What causes it?

Pumping groundwater can cause some underground layers to become more tightly packed. Natural settling can also contribute.

Explore Causes and Effects →
03

Why learn about Hagonoy?

Researchers have studied ground movement in Hagonoy and Calumpit and its connection with groundwater use and the growth of built-up areas.

View the Study Map →

Sources: NOAA, USGS, and Maclang et al. (2026) .
