What have you noticed in your area?

Have you noticed repeated flooding, uneven ground, or another environmental concern? Use this sample form to describe what you saw and where it happened.

Describe your observation

Sample form for our student project. You can try filling it out, but your answers will not be saved or sent.

Fields marked “required” need to be completed. Your name and the date are optional.

Please leave out private details, such as phone numbers, exact home addresses, or information about other people.

Not sure what to write?

Start with these details:

  • Where: Name the barangay or a nearby public landmark.
  • When: Include the date if you remember it.
  • What: Describe what you actually saw.
  • How often: Mention whether it happened once or has happened several times.

A simple example

“I noticed water covering part of the road near a public market after heavy rain. It made walking through the area difficult.”

This is an example only, not an actual report from a Hagonoy resident.
